##  Project Overview
This project analyzes coffee shop sales data from **January to June** to identify trends, top performing products and opportunities for revenue growth. The analysis was performed using:

- **MySQL** for data cleaning, querying and advanced calculations
- **Power BI** for interactive dashboards and visualization

---

##  Key Objectives
- Analyze **month-on-month performance** for sales and quantity
- Identify **top-selling products** by revenue and quantity
- Understand **customer behavior by day of week and time of day**
- Provide **data driven recommendations** to boost sales and profitability

---

##  Tools & Techniques
### **Power BI**
- I built an **interactive dashboard** to visualize KPIs:
  - Total Sales
  - Month-on-Month growth
  - Category performance
  - Store wise performance
  - Peak sales by hour/day

### **MySQL**
- **Created a database** and imported raw sales data
- **Converted text-based date and time to proper formats**
- Analyzed **sales trends by day of week**
- Queried **top 10 products by revenue and by quantity sold**
- Calculated **Month-on-Month Sales % and Quantity % growth** using the `LAG()` function

---

##  Key Insights (Jan–Jun)
- Sales grew consistently after February:
  - Jan → Feb: **-6.8%**
  - Feb → Mar: **+29.8%**
  - Mar → Apr: **+20.3%**
  - Apr → May: **+31.8%**
  - May → June: **+6.2%**
- **Coffee and Tea** contributed the highest revenue share **MoM**
- **Morning hours (8 AM–11 AM)** are peak sales periods
- Weekdays generated the most in total revenue while weekends underperformed
- Bakery and Drinking Chocolate categories were the highest contributors in terms of **revenue**
- Afternoon hours and weekends remain underutilized

---

##  Challenges Identified
- Afternoon slump (post 3 PM)
- Weekend revenue share stagnated despite overall growth
- Low-performing SKUs: packaged chocolate, flavored syrups

---

##  Recommendations
- **Afternoon Indulgence Deals** (2–5 PM) to boost slow hours
- **Weekend premium bundles** to improve weekend share
- Remove or repurpose **low-performing SKUs**
- Implement **loyalty programs** to increase repeat purchases
- Replicate best practices from **top-performing stores (Hell’s Kitchen)**

---

##  Why Use Both MySQL and Power BI?
- **MySQL** provided the **foundation for clean, accurate data** and allowed advanced calculations like **MoM percentage growth** and ranking queries.
- **Power BI** transformed these insights into **interactive dashboards** for **business stakeholders**, making complex data easy to understand and actionable.
- This combination demonstrates **end-to-end analytics skills** from **data engineering** to **data storytelling**.
